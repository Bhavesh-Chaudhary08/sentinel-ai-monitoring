Sentinel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Sentinel is a browser extension plus a dashboard used by organizations to monitor how generative-AI tools are used on devices they enroll. It is not installed by individuals for personal use: your organization installs it and tells you that it is installed.

What the extension collects

  • Prompt text you submit to AI tools on the sites your organization monitors (for example ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Copilot). A copy is stored, along with a hash and a redacted preview, so the dashboard can show what was sent.
  • AI tool usage metadata — which tool, which page, and when.
  • Visited domains that the extension recognizes as AI platforms, plus the page title of an unrecognized site when it looks like an AI chat interface. If extended detection is enabled in the extension's settings, other https sites are checked for an AI chat interface; a page is only reported when one is found.
  • Browser activity timing — active, idle, audible and away time per site, plus the page path, so the dashboard can show where working time went.
  • Account email and a device identifier, so captured activity can be attributed to the right person and device.

What it does not collect

The extension does not read or transmit passwords, form fields outside the monitored AI prompt boxes, keystrokes on other sites, your files, or activity in other browsers or applications. Capture does not run until you accept the monitoring notice on your account, and it stops when you sign out or disable the extension.

Where the data goes

Captured data is sent over HTTPS to the Sentinel server your organization configures — for this deployment, https://sentinel-7rlb.onrender.com — and stored in that organization's database. It is not sold, and it is not shared with third parties beyond the infrastructure used to run the service. Sensitive-value detection (credentials, PII, financial data) can run on your device before anything is uploaded, and prompts that are blocked never leave the browser.

How long it is kept

DataRetention
Browser time segments90 days
Prompts and interactions (unflagged)30 days
Prompts that triggered a DLP alert180 days

Your organization's administrator can change these periods; old rows are purged automatically.

Who can see it

You can always see your own data in My time and Weekly digest. Your organization's administrators can see per-person activity summaries and the prompts flagged by policy. Access is role-based and requires signing in.

Your visibility and control

  • The extension's popup always shows whether monitoring is on.
  • Monitoring requires your recorded consent; withdrawing it stops capture.
  • Signing out of the extension stops capture immediately.
